How Fieldcast compares

Three honest comparisons. Each one includes a section on when the other product is the better choice, because the alternative is a page nobody trusts.

  • Fieldcast vs Docparser — Parsing rules you configure per document layout, through a no-code interface.
  • Fieldcast vs Mindee — Pre-trained models per document type (invoice, receipt, passport, ID, bank statement), plus custom models for other types.
  • Fieldcast vs Nanonets — A workflow platform. Each step is a block — extraction, classification, routing, export — and you compose them.

Competitor figures were read from their own pricing pages and public sources in August 2026. If something here is wrong or out of date, email muhammet@decaylabs.online and it gets corrected.
