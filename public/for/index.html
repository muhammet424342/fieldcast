Who Fieldcast is for

Same endpoint in every case. What changes is the list of field names you send with the request.

  • Bookkeepers & accounts payable — Supplier invoices into your ledger, without a parser per supplier
  • Agencies & freelance developers — One endpoint you can resell across every client, whatever their documents look like
  • AI agent builders — A document-extraction tool your agent can call in one step

Each page includes a "what it does not do" section. Fieldcast is a beta with no OCR and no uptime guarantee, and it is better that you learn that here than after integrating.
